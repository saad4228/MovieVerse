# Product overview

MovieVerse unifies city-first, movie-first and cinema-first discovery with one booking flow. A selected screening binds its movie, city, cinema, format, language, price and physical auditorium. Visitors compare shows, explore a procedural cinema, preview a seat's view and reserve a group. Snacks, test checkout, downloadable QR tickets and account history complete the demonstration.

The signature experience is functional 3D seat selection backed by screening inventory. The same IDs and price calculation power the accessible 2D map. Users can always change city through the header. Navigation, search, language filters, date controls and order summaries are responsive.

Sample data and illustrative architecture are disclosed in the product. Only the open Sintel film has a configured clip; other movie trailers are replaceable fields. No live inventory, theatre partnerships, financial transactions or real admission rights are claimed.
