# Asset credits and replacement

- Current movie artwork uses original promotional images from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). Exact sources, dimensions, trailer links and channels are in `data/media-catalog.json`. This product uses TMDB imagery and is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Images remain the property of their rights holders; public availability does not establish a general commercial redistribution licence. The new posters and backdrops replace the first prototype's screenshot crops. `scripts/sync-hd-media.mjs` reproduces these assets without enlarging smaller originals.
- Commercial movie trailers remain official external links. Their identities are in `data/trailer-sources.json` and `data/media-catalog.json`. Streams are not extracted or embedded over the auditorium. No direct-video redistribution permission was established for these films.
- Powered by [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/): [Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07), [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor) and [Dirty Carpet](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirty_carpet). Local 2K colour, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license); sources are in `data/material-sources.json`. Fabric colour is neutralised to preserve seat class colours.
- Sintel is copyrighted by Blender Foundation (www.sintel.org), [CC BY 3.0](https://durian.blender.org/sharing/). The unchanged 1080p and 720p trailers come from [Blender's official download directory](https://download.blender.org/durian/trailer/); original end credits are retained. The poster and legacy 480p fallback came from the W3C media sample. The eight-second 360p HLS test fixture is a transcoded excerpt, never presented as another movie's trailer.
- Cinema and snack photographs were downloaded from Unsplash image CDN paths recorded in `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs`, under the Unsplash image license. Some product images are representative (for example the combo shares the popcorn photo). Replace product imagery through the catalog as desired.
- The auditorium feature image is derived from the supplied seats reference; it describes the visual goal and is explicitly labelled illustrative. Actual booking rooms are rendered from procedural geometry, not this image.
- DM Sans and Bebas Neue are locally bundled through Fontsource. License notices remain in their packages. UI icons use lucide-react.

City cinema directory sources and retrieval dates are recorded in `data/cinema-directory.json`. Directory entries are not verified film availability or exhaustive inventory.

All artwork/video URLs are configurable. Use the `trailers` array for playback sources and official external links. Direct trailer assets must be authorised and allow CORS when remote. Do not insert YouTube watch URLs into the MP4/VideoTexture field. See [integration configuration](INTEGRATIONS.md).
